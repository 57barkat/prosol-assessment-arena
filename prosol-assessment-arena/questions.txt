Candidate Practice Guide
Read every question carefully before looking at the options. Many reasoning questions are designed to test attention as much as knowledge.
For verbal questions, eliminate clearly wrong options first, then compare the remaining choices by meaning and context.
For analogy and series questions, identify the relationship or pattern before choosing an answer.
For maths logical questions, write the basic formula mentally: percentage, average, speed, ratio, profit/loss, work, or series pattern.
Practise in timed batches of 25 to 50 questions to build speed, accuracy and pressure handling for SSB and ISSB style screening.
